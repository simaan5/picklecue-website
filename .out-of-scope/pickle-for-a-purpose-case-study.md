# Present "Pickle for a Purpose" as a PickleCue case study or partnership

PickleCue promoted the Pickle for a Purpose charity tournament (Echelon LA Metro, 2026-08-29). It did not run it, and the site must not describe it as a PickleCue event, a case study, or a partnership.

## Why this is out of scope

Legal posture: a company that promotes a charity event and benefits from it can be a "commercial coventurer" under California law (§12599.2), which carries registration and contract duties; the agreement for this event was never signed. The Community page is deliberately worded around three limited things PickleCue does for such events and a "Nothing else" card. The event page is built from facts in `data/events.json` with no recap state, precisely so nobody is pressured into writing one.

## What would reopen it

A signed coventurer agreement for a specific future event, reviewed by counsel, and the organizer's written approval of the copy.
