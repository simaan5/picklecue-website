Orange County pickleball courts

November 3, 2026 · PickleCue

Four city pages in the directory: Lake Forest, Costa Mesa, Irvine, and Newport Beach. This is not a list of every Orange County city. Other cities have pages of their own, and some have none yet.

The number at the top of a city page counts court locations in that directory. A place that appears only under recorded details, with no court-page link, is a city listing and is not part of that count. Hours, fees, lights, and surfaces below are only the sentences those sources state.

Lake Forest

The Lake Forest page lists 5 court locations. 2 are free to play.

City of Lake Forest, Pickleball says Portola Park, 28040 Glenn Ranch Road, has three dedicated, lighted pickleball courts. The directory count of 3 is the dataset’s court count for OpenStreetMap way 1466666973. That way has no courts tag and no lit tag. The lights come from the city page.

The same city page says the 100 block of Walworth has two dedicated pickleball courts, privately maintained and available to the public. The directory lists 2 courts there, from way 1436792664.

Catalonia Park’s directory count is 2, from way 962996845, which is tagged surface=sand.

The city page says Lake Forest Recreation Center, 28000 Vista Terrace, has four indoor wooden courts, available from 8:30 a.m. to 10:30 a.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Fees on that page: residents $5 per visit or a $120 year pass; non-residents $10 per visit or a $240 year pass.

The same page lists two multi-use courts at Baker Ranch Park, 26380 Rancho Pkwy S, and says pickleball players must bring their own net and posts. It lists two multi-purpose courts each at Borrego Park (26982 Cabriole), Pittsford Park (21701 Pittsford Dr.), and Foothill Ranch Community Park (19422 Rue De Valore), and one multi-use court at Rimgate Park (29772 Rimgate Dr.). For those four it says players should bring their own nets and posts.

Costa Mesa

The Costa Mesa page lists 13 court locations. 1 is free to play.

City of Costa Mesa, Downtown Recreation Center gives the address as 1860 Anaheim Avenue, Costa Mesa, CA 92627, and the phone as (714) 327-7560. An open-gym table labeled Updated Hours (March–May) lists pickleball for ages 18+ (free for 50+) Tuesday through Friday, 8:15 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., and Saturday 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. Monday is marked N/A. The page says hours and days are subject to change and tells readers to call. It does not state a pickleball court count.

The directory count of 1 is the dataset’s court count for OpenStreetMap way 83201240, the recreation-center building, which has no courts tag.

Way 1471281635 is the directory row for Back Bay High School: 3 courts, surface=asphalt. Way 1541093856 is Charles W. TeWinkle Middle School: 2 courts, surface=asphalt, access=private. Way 1470146081 is Early College High School: 3 courts, surface=asphalt.

Irvine

The Irvine page lists 14 court locations. 7 are free to play. Mike Ward Community Park and Portola Springs Community Park are city listings on that page and are not part of the 14.

Way 1548498050 is the directory row for Dave Robins Park: 2 courts, lit=yes. Way 1222455443 is Odyssey Ropes Course: 4 courts, lit=yes, access=private. Way 766603501 is South Lake Park: lit=yes and sport=tennis;pickleball. The dataset has no court count for it, so the page states none.

City of Irvine, Mike Ward Community Park lists 8 lighted pickleball courts at 20 Lake Road, Irvine, CA 92604, about 22.0 acres, phone 949-724-6900, hours 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. A February 4, 2026 city article says the city opened eight new lighted courts there with a ribbon-cutting on February 3, and that four shade structures, picnic tables, benches, hydration stations, lighting improvements, restroom upgrades, landscape and irrigation work, and a sound-dampening barrier were part of the project.

City of Irvine, Pickleball says reservations are available for Mike Ward courts 3 and 4 and Portola Springs courts 5 and 6. Residents may book five days ahead, non-residents three days ahead, at yourirvine.org. The page lists $17 per hour for residents and $19 per hour for non-residents, one or two hours, one reservation per person per day, Monday through Sunday from 7 a.m. to noon and from 4 p.m. to 10 p.m. Those courts can be used drop-in when they are not reserved or a reservation does not show. Reservations are nonrefundable except for inclement weather, and they are for non-commercial use. The page lists 949-724-6417 and pickleball@cityofirvine.org for general pickleball questions.

City of Irvine, Portola Springs Community Park lists 6 lighted pickleball courts at 900 Tomato Springs, Irvine, CA 92620, about 32 acres, phone 949-724-6192, hours 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. OpenStreetMap way 1081065636 names the park, operator City of Irvine, at 900 Tomato Springs, Irvine, CA 92620, and has no sport, courts, or lit tag. The same pickleball page says there are 10 hybrid tennis and pickleball courts spread across Heritage, Knollcrest, Los Olivos, Portola Springs, San Carlo, Turtle Rock, and University. It does not say how many of the 10 are at Portola Springs. A directory page titled Pickleball Courts at Portola Springs Community Center is filed under Garden Grove with a count of 8. That count is not the city’s figure.

Newport Beach

The Newport Beach page lists 9 court locations. 2 are free to play. Newport Coast Community Center is a city listing and is not part of the 9.

The city facility sheet for Bonita Canyon Sports Park lists a pickleball court at 1990 Ford Road and does not give a number. The directory lists 4 courts for Bonita Canyon Sports Park West, from way 1412297450.

Way 1520719297 is the directory row for Mariners Park: 8 courts, surface=asphalt, access=private.

City of Newport Beach, Newport Coast Community Center says the gymnasium floor is lined for basketball, volleyball, and pickleball, at 6401 San Joaquin Hills Road. It does not say how many pickleball courts the lines mark.
