How to run a pickleball round robin

November 3, 2026 · PickleCue

A round robin means every side plays every other side once. The organizer template on this site is a spreadsheet builder for that shape of event. It does not invent a venue, a fee, or a list of who is already booked.

What you decide first

The builder asks for a format. The choices are mixed doubles round robin, men’s doubles round robin, women’s doubles round robin, and singles round robin. Doubles counts two players per team. Singles counts one.

You set the number of teams, from 2 up to 64. The page says PickleCue currently supports up to 64 teams per imported tournament. You set how many courts you have, from 1 to 32, and the first court number, so a facility numbered 3 through 11 can start at 3.

The summary on the page computes a full round robin as one fewer round than the team count when that count is even, and as the same number as the team count when it is odd. The line under that figure says everyone plays everyone once. The rounds field itself accepts 1 to 40, so you can run fewer rounds than a full round robin. Points to win are 11, 15, or 21. Win by is 1 or 2.

Games per round in the summary is the smaller of the court count and half the teams, rounded down. If you enter more courts than that, the page warns that only half the teams, rounded down, can play at once.

What the blank sheet contains

Download runs in the browser. Nothing is uploaded to make the file. The blank file has rows for each team, a row for each court you numbered, and one match row per court per round. Those match rows do not name the two sides. You assign who plays whom when you fill the sheet in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets. Do not rearrange the section rows. The page says those rows are how PickleCue reads the file. If you edit in Excel or Google Sheets, export a CSV before you import.

Score columns, a standings table, a top-teams block, and a public preview are optional. Leaving a section out does not break the import. The top-teams block on a blank file has a row per rank up to four, and never more teams than you entered. Print settings cover letter, legal, and tabloid, portrait or landscape as listed on the page.

There is also a filled 18-team example download. The page labels it a generated example. The names and scores in that file are not a real event.

Import, then preview

On the organizers page, creating the competition is the first step. In the app the template page says to open Compete, then Organizer, then Create Event, then Import Spreadsheet. You get a preview that flags anything the file needs, in plain language. The page says previews and dry runs are free for everyone, and creating the live tournament is part of PickleCue Pro.

Pick a court from the court directory before you print the schedule. The directory tells you where a location is. It does not tell you that the courts are reserved for your event. If you want a place marked free to play, the free-courts checklist is the page that explains that label.
