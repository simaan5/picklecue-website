Pickle for a Purpose

A charity pickleball evening that brings players together and supports a local cause.

  • Saturday, August 29, 2026 · 5–8 PM
  • Peninsula Racquet Club, 30850 Hawthorne Blvd, Rancho Palos Verdes · Get directions
  • Organized by Echelon LA Metro
View official event page

Registration is currently unavailable on the organizer’s site. The event is still scheduled. Registration and payment are handled entirely on official event page ↗, and only they can confirm whether it reopens.

What to expect

  • Mixed doubles
  • All skill levels
  • Register solo or bring a partner
  • Pro coaching
  • Beginner clinic
  • Awards & raffle
  • Networking mixer
  • Snacks & non‑alcoholic drinks
No partner? No problem.

Register solo and the organizers will pair you by skill level, or bring a partner.

Player registration includes

  • Club access & tournament play
  • Pro coaching & beginner instruction
  • Rental paddles and balls
  • Welcome bag, refreshments & mixer
  • Medal eligibility

General admission includes

  • Club access & courtside seating
  • Beginner instruction
  • Welcome bag, refreshments & mixer

Published prices

Players $100per player Set by the organizer
General admission $75per person Set by the organizer

Registration is currently unavailable on the organizer’s site. These are the prices they last published, shown so you know what the event costs if registration reopens. We are not the organizer and cannot take a registration — check their page before you travel.

The organizers state that event proceeds support Salvation Army LA Metro programs serving local communities.

PickleCue is supporting this event through community promotion only. PickleCue is not affiliated with Echelon LA Metro or The Salvation Army, does not solicit or accept donations, and receives no funds from this event. Registration, payment, operations, policies, and attendance are handled entirely by the organizers on their own platform; statements about how event proceeds are used are the organizers’.

Organized by Echelon LA Metro

The young-professionals chapter of The Salvation Army in Los Angeles. Pickle for a Purpose is their first annual charity pickleball tournament.
