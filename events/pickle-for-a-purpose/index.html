Community event supported by PickleCue

Pickle for a Purpose

PickleCue is helping spotlight a community event that brings players together and supports a local cause.

  • Saturday, August 29, 2026
  • 5–8 PM
  • Peninsula Racquet Club30850 Hawthorne Blvd, Rancho Palos Verdes, CA
  • Event organizer: Echelon LA Metro
Register Event details

What to expect

  • Mixed doubles
  • All skill levels
  • Register solo or bring a partner
  • Pro coaching
  • Beginner clinic
  • Awards & raffle
  • Networking mixer
  • Light snacks & non‑alcoholic beverages

You don’t need a partner and you don’t need experience — register on your own and the organizers will pair you by skill level, or bring someone along. Player registration includes club access, pro coaching during the event, the beginner clinic, rental paddles and balls, a welcome bag, medal eligibility, refreshments, and the networking mixer. General admission includes club access, beginner instruction, courtside spectator access, a welcome bag, refreshments, and the mixer.

Register for Pickle for a Purpose

PickleCue is supporting this event through community promotion. This event is organized by Echelon LA Metro. Registration, payment, event operations, policies, and attendance are handled by the event organizer.

About Echelon LA Metro

Echelon LA Metro is the young-professionals chapter of The Salvation Army in Los Angeles — a group of people in their 20s and 30s who love this city and want to give back to it. Pickle for a Purpose is their first annual charity pickleball tournament. Follow the organizers on Instagram: @echelonlosangeles.
