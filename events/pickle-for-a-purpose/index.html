Community event supported by PickleCue

Pickle for a Purpose

Pickleball, community, and a cause worth playing for. PickleCue is proud to support Echelon LA Metro's first annual charity pickleball tournament, benefiting The Salvation Army's programs across Los Angeles County.

Register for the tournament Event details

When
Saturday, August 29, 2026 · 5:00–8:00 PM
Where
Peninsula Racquet Club30850 Hawthorne Blvd, Rancho Palos Verdes, CA
Parking
Free, off Crestmont Lane
Format
Mixed doublesBeginner through advanced — register solo and be paired, or bring your own partner
Benefiting
The Salvation Army LA MetroFood assistance, housing support, youth and senior services across Los Angeles County

Everyone is welcome

You don't need a partner and you don't need experience. Register on your own and the organizers will pair you up, or bring someone along. There's a basics clinic for anyone newer to the game, and pro coaching on court throughout the evening.

On the court

Mixed doubles across every skill level, with professional coaching and a beginner basics clinic.

Off the court

A networking reception, raffle prizes throughout the night, and a welcome bag for every attendee.

Awards

Recognition for 1st, 2nd and 3rd place — plus a Top Fundraising Team award.

Player registration includes club access, pro coaching during the event, the beginner clinic, rental paddles and balls, a welcome bag, medal eligibility, refreshments, and the networking mixer. General admission includes club access, beginner instruction, courtside spectator access, a welcome bag, refreshments, and the mixer.

Register for Pickle for a Purpose

This event is organized by Echelon LA Metro in support of The Salvation Army. Registration, payments, refunds, and event operations are managed entirely by the event organizers. PickleCue is supporting the event through community promotion.

About Echelon LA Metro

Echelon LA Metro is the young-professionals chapter of The Salvation Army in Los Angeles — a group of people in their 20s and 30s who love this city and want to give back to it. Pickle for a Purpose is their first annual charity pickleball tournament. Follow the organizers on Instagram: @echelonlosangeles.

Running an event of your own?

PickleCue helps clubs, nonprofits, and tournament directors organize games, leagues, and tournaments — and reach the players nearby who want to show up. If you're planning something for your community, we'd love to hear about it.
