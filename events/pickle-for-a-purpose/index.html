Pickle for a Purpose

A charity pickleball evening that brings players together and supports a local cause.

  • Saturday, August 29, 2026 · 5–8 PM
  • Peninsula Racquet Club, 30850 Hawthorne Blvd, Rancho Palos Verdes · Get directions
  • Organized by Echelon LA Metro
Register for the event

Registration and payment are handled on The Salvation Army’s official event page ↗

What to expect

  • Mixed doubles
  • All skill levels
  • Register solo or bring a partner
  • Pro coaching
  • Beginner clinic
  • Awards & raffle
  • Networking mixer
  • Snacks & non‑alcoholic drinks
No partner? No problem.

Register solo and the organizers will pair you by skill level, or bring a partner.

Player registration includes

  • Club access & tournament play
  • Pro coaching & beginner instruction
  • Rental paddles and balls
  • Welcome bag, refreshments & mixer
  • Medal eligibility

General admission includes

  • Club access & courtside seating
  • Beginner instruction
  • Welcome bag, refreshments & mixer
