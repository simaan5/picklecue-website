PickleCue in the community

Pickleball that brings people together.

Pickleball is more than a game. It's a way to build friendships, support real causes, and strengthen a neighborhood. PickleCue supports clubs, nonprofits, tournament organizers, and community leaders doing exactly that.

Events we support

Charity tournaments, fundraisers, and community events we're helping spread the word about.

Community event supported by PickleCue

Pickle for a Purpose

Saturday, August 29, 2026 · Rancho Palos Verdes

Echelon LA Metro's first annual charity pickleball tournament, benefiting The Salvation Army's food assistance, housing, youth, and senior programs across Los Angeles County. Mixed doubles, beginner through advanced.

View event Register

Organized by Echelon LA Metro in support of The Salvation Army. Registration, payments, and event operations are handled by the organizers — PickleCue is helping promote it.
