PickleCue in the Community

Some of the best pickleball happens around a cause. When a club or a nonprofit puts one on, we help people find it.

What “supported by PickleCue” means

Three things, and only these three. An organizer should be able to read this and know exactly what they are getting and what they still own.

  • A page for the event

    Date, venue, what it includes, and the prices the organizer published — taken from their own listing and re-checked against it. Every button sends people to the organizer’s page to register.

  • Assets they can post

    Social cards, stories and a printable poster in the event’s own branding, ready to hand to whoever runs the account. They are the organizer’s to use however they like.

  • Nothing else

    We do not take registrations, hold money, set prices, or run anything on the day. We are not affiliated with the organizations we promote, we do not solicit or accept donations, and we receive no funds from their events.
