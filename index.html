Discover

Where can I play?

A searchable map of public courts, with the detail that decides whether you drive there — surface, lights, indoor or out — and the courts you keep coming back to saved for next time.

Games

Who can I play with?

Post an open game or join one. Everyone sees the same roster, the same time and the same court — so nobody drives out to a game that quietly fell apart.

Scoring

Keep every point.

Real scoring, live everywhere. One person keeps score and the number moves at the same moment on the players' phones, the spectators' browsers, the board at the venue, and the Lock Screen.

After the match

What happens afterward?

Scores become a record you can actually read, and leagues and tournaments keep themselves current — standings recalculate, brackets advance the winner for you.

Organizers

Can it run the entire event?

Open a laptop at the desk. Every match on one screen — update a live score, correct a finished one, hand a court to a scorekeeper, print the QR, export the day.

Registration, waiver, pairing, check-in, scoring, bracket, champion — one event, end to end.

Spectators

Can everyone follow without installing anything?

Most people at an event will never install an app. Print the QR, or say the short code out loud. They get the schedule, their next match, the live score and the bracket — in a browser.
