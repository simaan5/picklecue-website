Frequently asked questions

When does PickleCue launch?

PickleCue for iOS is in final App Review. Join the waitlist and we will email you the day it is live on the App Store.

Is PickleCue free?

Yes. Finding courts, joining games, logging matches, and messaging are free. An optional subscription, PickleCue Pro, adds organizer tools like unlimited game creation, leagues, and tournaments.

Which devices does it run on?

PickleCue is a native iPhone app for iOS 18 and later, and it runs on iPad too.

Do you show ads or sell my data?

No ads, no data sold, no tracking across apps. Read the full privacy policy.

Do organizers need Pro?

Creating leagues and tournaments is part of PickleCue Pro. Players join games, leagues, and tournaments for free.

Can spectators follow matches without the app?

Yes. Live scores and brackets have share pages that open in any browser. Try the live demo.
