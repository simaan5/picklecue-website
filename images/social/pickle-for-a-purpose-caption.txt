Pickleball with a purpose. 💚🏓

PickleCue is proud to support Echelon LA Metro (@echelonlosangeles) and its first annual Pickle for a Purpose charity pickleball tournament—bringing players together for an evening of competition, community, and giving back.

Whether you're brand new to pickleball or an experienced player, everyone is welcome.

🏓 Mixed doubles
🤝 Register solo or bring your own partner
📈 Beginner through advanced skill levels
🎓 Pro coaching + beginner clinic
🏆 Awards & raffle prizes
🎁 Welcome bags
🥤 Light snacks & non-alcoholic beverages
🤝 Networking mixer

No partner? No problem. Register individually and the organizers will pair you based on skill level.

The organizers state that event proceeds support Salvation Army LA Metro programs serving communities throughout Los Angeles.
