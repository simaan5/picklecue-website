Pickleball with a purpose. 💚🏓

We're excited to support Echelon LA Metro's first annual Pickle for a Purpose charity pickleball tournament — Saturday, August 29 at Peninsula Racquet Club in Rancho Palos Verdes.

Whether you're picking up a paddle for the first time or already competing, everyone is welcome.

🏓 Mixed doubles, beginner through advanced
🤝 Register solo and get paired by skill level — or bring a partner
🎓 Pro coaching on court all evening
🌱 Beginner basics clinic before tournament play
🏆 Awards for 1st, 2nd, 3rd & Top Fundraising Team
🎟️ Raffle prizes throughout the night
🎁 Welcome bags, refreshments & a networking mixer

Proceeds support The Salvation Army's Los Angeles-area programs — food assistance, housing support, youth and senior services.
